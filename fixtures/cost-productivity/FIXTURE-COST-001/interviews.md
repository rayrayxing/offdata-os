# Synthetic stakeholder interviews

## Chief Executive Officer

The CEO expects a rapid ten percent cost reduction and believes supervisor spans and automation can provide most of it. The target was set before demand, task and capacity analysis.

## Chief Operating Officer

The COO states that travel, emergency coverage, rework and schedule volatility drive labour demand. A uniform utilisation target would remove the buffer needed for weather events and emergency jobs.

## Chief Financial Officer

The CFO distinguishes gross capacity, avoidable cost, cost avoidance and cash-releasing savings. No positions are approved for removal, and allocated technology and property costs cannot be assumed to disappear.

## Head of Safety

The safety lead requires minimum response coverage, fatigue controls and verified training compliance. The current training denominator is not comparable across countries.
