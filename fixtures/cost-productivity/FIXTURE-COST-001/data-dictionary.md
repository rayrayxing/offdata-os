# COST-001 data dictionary

- `direct_labour_cost`: payroll and contractor field-operations cost.
- `productive_hours`: estimated customer-value and required control work; travel and rework coding is incomplete.
- `paid_hours`: paid available hours before leave and absence adjustments.
- `minimum_resilience_capacity`: capacity buffer required for emergency coverage and continuity.
- `addressable_spend`: supplier spend potentially open to specification, demand or commercial intervention.
- `avoidable_cost`: working estimate of cost that can be removed, not allocated accounting cost.
- `automation_capacity`: gross hours potentially released before adoption, exception handling and demand growth.
- `approved_position_removals`: positions approved through a governed workforce plan.

Known limitations: overtime causation is partly sampled; cancellation definitions conflict; training compliance lacks a comparable denominator; inventory evidence is stale; no employment-law or supplier-negotiation evidence is included.
