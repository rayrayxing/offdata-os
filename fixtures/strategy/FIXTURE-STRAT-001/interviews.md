# Synthetic stakeholder interviews

## Group CEO

The CEO argues that all five businesses reinforce the group brand and that the digital-maintenance unit will become the next growth engine. No quantified parenting advantage or customer cross-sell evidence was supplied.

## Group CFO

The CFO states that the SGD 95 million capital ceiling is binding, leverage headroom is limited and legacy engineering has consumed cash in four of the last five years. Corporate allocations would not all disappear after a disposal.

## Legacy Engineering President

The president claims the unit earns an 8 percent return using replacement value rather than the finance capital base, and says a SGD 35 million plant renewal will restore competitiveness. The proposal contains no customer commitments.

## Independent Director

The director asks for explicit invest, restructure, partner, divest and close alternatives, a downside scenario, a maximum capital-at-risk view and a record of which management claims remain unverified.
