# STRAT-001 data dictionary

- `revenue`: reported business-line revenue in SGD.
- `ebitda_margin`: EBITDA divided by revenue, in percentage points.
- `roic`: operating return divided by the finance invested-capital base; missing for digital maintenance.
- `market_growth`: estimated annual market growth for 2027–2029.
- `capital_request`: management-requested gross growth or renewal capital for 2027–2029.
- `quality_status`: `established`, `estimated`, `contradictory`, `stale` or `missing`.

Known limitations: market estimates are not independently validated; corporate allocations are not avoidability estimates; customer concentration and scenario downside are incomplete; no legal, tax or transaction evidence is included.
