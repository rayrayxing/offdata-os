# 01 — Product Vision

## Vision

Build offdata into a persistent, Founder-governed consulting operating system that can originate, frame, execute, quality-assure and support implementation of consulting engagements while preserving human accountability for material decisions and relationships.

## Primary outcome

A single Founder should be able to operate with the analytical and delivery leverage of a high-quality consulting team, including analyst, consultant and engagement-management capacity, without pretending that judgement, trust, accountability and client politics can be fully automated.

## Target operating boundary

### offdata should automate or substantially assist

- Opportunity monitoring and preparation
- Mandate conversion and engagement framing
- Problem decomposition and hypothesis management
- Research planning and evidence collection
- Method selection and analysis execution
- Quantitative modelling and value cases
- Interview and workshop preparation
- Synthesis, storyline and recommendation drafting
- PPTX, DOCX, XLSX, PDF, SVG and HTML production
- Infographics and decision visualisations
- Project management, status reporting and follow-up
- Independent quality review and defect repair
- Implementation planning and benefits tracking
- Methodology scouting and candidate preparation

### The Founder remains accountable for

- Client trust and relationship development
- Material scoping and commercial choices
- Final recommendations
- Sensitive stakeholder judgement
- Negotiation and commitments
- Legal, regulatory and professional sign-off
- External outreach policy and campaign approval
- Production release and risk acceptance

## Core product surfaces

1. **Founder Cockpit** — decision inbox, exceptions, approvals, engagement status and economics.
2. **Engagement Workspace** — mandate, hypotheses, evidence, methods, models, recommendations and delivery state.
3. **Research and Evidence Room** — sources, claims, gaps, contradictions and provenance.
4. **Quant and Value Studio** — reproducible models, scenarios, sensitivities and reconciliations.
5. **Story and Deliverable Studio** — canonical story model and multi-format rendering.
6. **CRM and Origination Workspace** — accounts, contacts, opportunities, triggers and approved outreach.
7. **Methodology Library** — structured methods, compatibility, evidence requirements and quality tests.
8. **Methodology Radar** — scheduled discovery and governed promotion of new candidate methods.
9. **Implementation and Benefits Workspace** — initiatives, adoption, outcomes, owners and verified benefits.
10. **Quality and Assurance Console** — defects, independent reviews, thresholds and release gates.

## Engagement coverage

The initial design must support:

- Corporate and business-unit strategy
- Growth and commercial strategy
- Cost and productivity
- Customer experience
- Operating-model transformation
- Organisation and workforce
- Digital and AI transformation
- Risk and controls
- M&A and post-merger integration
- Carve-out and separation
- IPO, valuation and capital strategy
- Implementation and change
- Benefits realisation and performance improvement

Compound engagements must be supported without duplicating engagement state.

## Quality ambition

The target is not generic AI output. The target is work that is:

- Decision-led
- Evidence-grounded
- Hypothesis-driven
- Quantitatively reproducible
- Explicit about uncertainty
- Internally traceable
- Visually professional
- Consistent across deliverables
- Implementable
- Independently reviewed

## Expected workload reduction

Planning objective for a mature system:

- 70–85% reduction in production and engagement-management burden for analytically driven work
- 80–90% reduction for repeatable diagnostics or audits
- Lower automation for relationship management, negotiation, political judgement and final accountability

These are design targets, not guaranteed performance claims. They must be measured through pilot engagements.

## Product principles

- One consulting kernel, dynamically loaded domain capability packs
- Minimum sufficient method stack, not framework accumulation
- One canonical engagement model, many output formats
- Human interruption only for material decisions and exceptions
- Replaceable models, runtimes and worker harnesses
- Local-first development and controlled cloud progression
- Singapore-first data residency with future regional cells
- Internal evidence richness with proportionate client-facing citation
- Continuous learning through governed candidate promotion, not uncontrolled self-modification
