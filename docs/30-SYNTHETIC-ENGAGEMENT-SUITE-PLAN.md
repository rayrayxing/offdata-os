# 30 — Synthetic Engagement Suite Plan

## Status

Canonical fixture programme v1.0. The suite is the principal proof that offdata can perform consulting work, not merely run software.

## 1. Fixture purpose

Synthetic engagements must test the complete consulting chain:

`opportunity → mandate → framing → evidence → hypotheses → method selection → analysis → recommendation → quality → deliverables → implementation → outcomes → benefits`

Fixtures are fictional and evaluative. They are not canned answers and must not be treated as real client evidence.

## 2. Fixture construction standard

Every primary fixture contains:

### Client and mandate

- fictional organisation profile;
- sector and geographic context;
- decision owner and stakeholders;
- initial conversations or notes;
- incomplete mandate;
- explicit scope, exclusions and constraints;
- intended decision date;
- commercial and delivery assumptions.

### Evidence pack

- interview transcripts;
- policies, reports, presentations and emails;
- structured operational and financial datasets;
- source-document metadata and exact passages;
- evidence-quality variation;
- deliberate contradiction;
- missing and stale information;
- irrelevant but tempting material;
- one or more malicious or untrusted-input cases.

### Analytical oracle

- expected decision framing;
- acceptable problem archetypes;
- minimum method stack;
- acceptable alternative methods;
- methods that should be rejected and why;
- expected calculations and tolerances;
- expected confidence and uncertainty;
- required specialist review;
- expected Founder decisions and escalation packets.

### Delivery oracle

- governing thought and proposition hierarchy;
- expected slide and report structure;
- named model outputs;
- visual archetypes;
- citation mode;
- expected implementation initiatives;
- benefit measures and verification thresholds.

### Defect pack

- unsupported claim;
- citation-scope error;
- numerical mismatch;
- hidden assumption;
- inappropriate method;
- missing alternative;
- weak implementation ownership;
- premature benefit claim;
- visual clipping or inconsistency;
- authority or confidentiality violation.

## 3. Primary fixtures

## FIXTURE-STRAT-001 — Corporate portfolio and capital allocation

### Decision

Which business lines should receive growth capital, be restructured, partnered, divested or closed over the next three years?

### Core tests

- corporate advantage and parenting logic;
- market and business economics;
- scenario and option value;
- capital allocation;
- strategic coherence and implementation.

### Deliberate traps

- revenue growth presented without return or cash economics;
- attractive market but structurally weak company position;
- management preference disguised as fact;
- unrelated diversification benefits overstated.

## FIXTURE-GROWTH-001 — Growth engine and commercial redesign

### Decision

Which customer segments, offers, prices and routes to market should receive investment to restore profitable growth?

### Core tests

- revenue decomposition;
- market sizing;
- customer needs and segmentation;
- pricing and elasticity;
- funnel and channel economics;
- experiments and staged investment.

### Deliberate traps

- total market confused with accessible demand;
- average customer economics hiding loss-making cohorts;
- correlation between campaign exposure and purchase treated as causation;
- discounting recommended without elasticity evidence.

## FIXTURE-COST-001 — Cost and productivity transformation

### Decision

Where can recurring cost be removed without damaging service, safety, resilience or future capability?

### Core tests

- cost attribution;
- demand, process and capacity diagnosis;
- productivity comparison;
- scenario and implementation economics;
- cash-releasing versus capacity and cost avoidance.

### Deliberate traps

- allocated overhead treated as avoidable;
- headcount reduction before demand and process analysis;
- utilisation target that reduces resilience;
- benefits double counted across initiatives.

## FIXTURE-CX-001 — Customer journey and service redesign

### Decision

Which journeys should be redesigned first and what changes will improve customer outcomes and economics?

### Core tests

- customer and journey prioritisation;
- behavioural and operational evidence;
- service blueprint;
- accessibility and fairness;
- retention and economic impact;
- causal evaluation.

### Deliberate traps

- satisfaction score treated as sufficient diagnosis;
- vocal-customer bias;
- frontstage redesign without backstage feasibility;
- retention claim without cohort or counterfactual evidence.

## FIXTURE-OM-001 — Target operating model

### Decision

How should capabilities, processes, organisation, decision rights, sourcing, technology and performance management be configured to execute strategy?

### Core tests

- capability and value-stream design;
- organisation and decision rights;
- service-delivery model;
- technology and data requirements;
- governance and performance routines;
- sequencing and feasibility.

### Deliberate traps

- universal target-operating-model template;
- organisation chart designed before work and decisions;
- centralisation recommended without economics or control rationale;
- technology platform treated as the operating model.

## FIXTURE-WF-001 — Workforce strategy and organisation redesign

### Decision

What work, roles, skills, capacity and organisation will be required under future demand and AI scenarios?

### Core tests

- task and process analysis;
- workforce demand and supply scenarios;
- skills and critical-role analysis;
- organisation design;
- spans and layers;
- fairness and transition.

### Deliberate traps

- roles automated without task-level analysis;
- productivity assumption applied uniformly;
- contractor and attrition dynamics ignored;
- efficiency recommendation creating control or knowledge risk.

## FIXTURE-DAI-001 — SME AI opportunity, value and risk audit

### Decision

Which AI-enabled use cases should the fictional SME fund, experiment with, prepare, defer or reject, and what foundations and controls are required?

### Core tests

- transformation ambition and strategic focus;
- process and decision opportunity identification;
- use-case value, feasibility and risk;
- data, technology and workforce readiness;
- experimentation and scaling;
- AI governance and benefits.

### Deliberate traps

- vendor claims treated as evidence;
- automation of a poorly defined process;
- labour saving assumed to become cash benefit;
- sensitive use case prioritised without governance;
- chatbot proposed where search or process redesign is superior.

## FIXTURE-RISK-001 — Enterprise risk and control redesign

### Decision

Which risks and controls require remediation, redesign, assurance or acceptance, and where should limited investment be directed?

### Core tests

- objective and risk mechanism;
- causal events and control barriers;
- design and operating effectiveness;
- residual exposure;
- scenario and quantitative estimation;
- assurance and accountability.

### Deliberate traps

- heat map used as final analysis;
- control existence treated as effectiveness;
- certification treated as complete assurance;
- risk transfer assumed to remove operational exposure.

## FIXTURE-MA-001 — Acquisition and integration

### Decision

Should the buyer proceed, at what maximum price and under what integration and value-realisation conditions?

### Core tests

- strategic alternatives and incremental value;
- commercial and operational diligence;
- stand-alone and combination economics;
- synergy evidence and price ceiling;
- regulatory and execution risk;
- integration and benefit governance.

### Deliberate traps

- revenue synergy without customer evidence;
- all cost synergy treated as cash releasing;
- maximum price mechanically based on average multiples;
- integration costs and dis-synergies omitted.

## FIXTURE-CARVE-001 — Carve-out and separation

### Decision

What perimeter, transition services, duplicated capabilities and separation sequence are required to transfer a viable business?

### Core tests

- perimeter and dependency mapping;
- stranded cost and stand-up cost;
- systems, data, contracts and people separation;
- TSA design and exit;
- operational continuity and controls;
- benefit and dis-benefit tracking.

### Deliberate traps

- legal perimeter confused with operational viability;
- shared systems separated without data lineage;
- stranded costs assumed to disappear immediately;
- TSA exit dates unsupported by capability milestones.

## FIXTURE-IPO-001 — IPO readiness, valuation and capital strategy

### Decision

Should the company pursue a public-market pathway now, what valuation range is supportable and what readiness gaps must be resolved?

### Core tests

- strategic financing alternatives;
- public-company readiness;
- valuation methods and reconciliation;
- investor expectations and reverse valuation;
- financing resilience and downside;
- specialist legal, audit and valuation boundaries.

### Deliberate traps

- mechanical average of valuation methods;
- selected peers with structurally different economics;
- legal and accounting conclusions made by AI;
- readiness reduced to exchange eligibility.

## FIXTURE-CHANGE-001 — Implementation and adoption

### Decision

Should a major change be authorised, redesigned, piloted, scaled or stopped, and what implementation system is required?

### Core tests

- theory of change;
- readiness and determinant diagnosis;
- behavioural analysis;
- implementation strategy;
- process and failure risk;
- adoption, outcome and sustainment evaluation.

### Deliberate traps

- communication plan treated as change strategy;
- training completion treated as adoption;
- intervention failure and implementation failure confused;
- rollout scaled without pilot evidence.

## FIXTURE-BEN-001 — Benefits realisation and performance improvement

### Decision

Which interventions should be scaled, adapted or stopped, and which benefits can be declared realised?

### Core tests

- benefit dependency and ownership;
- performance diagnosis;
- baseline and counterfactual;
- causal attribution;
- economic valuation;
- persistence and scale.

### Deliberate traps

- output or milestone labelled as benefit;
- normal variation treated as improvement;
- gross benefit without leakage and cost;
- double counting between initiatives;
- benefit declared before persistence evidence.

## 5. Fixture difficulty levels

Each fixture should have three configurations:

- `foundation` — clear but incomplete mandate; moderate ambiguity.
- `advanced` — conflicting stakeholders, data defects and mixed methods.
- `adversarial` — malicious input, misleading evidence, permission traps and severe uncertainty.

## 6. Expected-answer design

Avoid one exact prose answer. Store:

- mandatory conclusions;
- prohibited conclusions;
- acceptable alternatives;
- required evidence links;
- numerical expected values and tolerance;
- required uncertainty statements;
- required escalation;
- optional enhancements;
- quality threshold by assurance tier.

## 7. Synthetic-data generation rules

- Use fixed random seeds.
- Store generation scripts and produced data.
- Preserve raw and transformed datasets separately.
- Create realistic but fictional identifiers and names.
- Do not use real personal data.
- Include deliberate missingness and outliers with a documented purpose.
- Include units, time periods and data dictionaries.
- Keep an answer-key version separate from agent-visible inputs.

## 8. Evaluation modes

### Deterministic mode

Tests schemas, transitions, calculations, reconciliation and permissions.

### Agent component mode

Tests one specialist agent against a bounded subset.

### Workstream mode

Tests multiple agents through one analytical workstream.

### Full engagement mode

Tests complete lifecycle, artefacts and Founder decisions.

### Mutation mode

Alters one source, assumption, value or policy and verifies appropriate regression and repair.

## 9. Founder review sample

The Founder should manually review:

- all first-time fixture baselines;
- any material change to expected recommendation;
- all visual golden outputs;
- all external-action and origination cases;
- a rotating sample of recurring regression results.

## 10. Build order

1. Complete `FIXTURE-DAI-001` foundation pack.
2. Use it to validate the full data and evaluation schema.
3. Build `FIXTURE-STRAT-001` and `FIXTURE-COST-001` to test different analytical patterns.
4. Build remaining primary fixtures.
5. Build compound fixtures.
6. Add adversarial variants.
7. Establish automated mutation and regression generation.

## 11. Completion gate

The synthetic suite is ready for a controlled real-client pilot only when:

- all thirteen primary fixtures pass mandatory deterministic and agent tests;
- at least three compound fixtures pass end to end;
- all external-action controls pass;
- cross-format outputs reconcile;
- material calculations reproduce;
- recovery and retry tests pass;
- no fixture leaks answer-key content into agent inputs;
- Founder acceptance is recorded.
