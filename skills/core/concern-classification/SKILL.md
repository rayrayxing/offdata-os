# Concern Classification

Use deterministic policy semantics:
- BLOCKING: cannot responsibly proceed;
- MATERIAL_EXPERIMENT: important uncertainty; buy evidence;
- DESIGN_CONSTRAINT: shape solution/operation;
- MONITOR: retain visibly, no current block.

Provide rationale/evidence/confidence. Concerns persist until resolved/accepted/invalidated/superseded.
