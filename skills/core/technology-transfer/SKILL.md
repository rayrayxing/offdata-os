# Technology Transfer

For a FrontierSignal generate a disciplined opportunity matrix: capability × industry, job role, workflow, customer type, regulation, business model and distribution channel. Prioritize contexts where the capability changes cost/time/risk/quality structurally. Preserve weak/unusual hypotheses for EIR review rather than prematurely converging.
