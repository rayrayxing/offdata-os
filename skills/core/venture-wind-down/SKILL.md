# Wind-down

Stop campaigns/new commitments, revoke unnecessary authority, cancel unneeded infrastructure, honor customer obligations, collect receivables/process authorized refunds, preserve required data, close/reconcile finance, capture final economics, produce kill AAR/learning candidates and release capital. A kill is complete only when external commitments/resources are reconciled.
