# Context Recombination

Methods: recombine capabilities; invert who performs workflow; eliminate rather than optimize workflow; convert consulting→software, software→autonomous service, employee task→API; move enterprise capability to SMB/professional/regulated/underserved context; explore embedded distribution. Output hypotheses with novelty rationale and critical uncertainties, not fake market facts.
