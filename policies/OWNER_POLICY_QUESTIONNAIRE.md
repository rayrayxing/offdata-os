# Owner Capital / Risk Policy Questionnaire

Ask and record explicit answers before LIVE capital/contact authority:

- Base currency?
- Total experimental capital available?
- Maximum capital exposure per venture?
- Maximum single experiment spend?
- Maximum autonomous spend per action/day?
- Spend level requiring owner approval?
- Maximum autonomous customer sends/day and per-domain/contact frequency?
- May agents buy SaaS? Register domains? Provision cloud? Buy ads?
- Discount/refund authority?
- Which actions always require owner approval?
- Portfolio drawdown or loss limit causing automatic pause?
- Prohibited industries, business models, geographies, actions or data classes?
- Minimum cash reserve?
- Maximum concurrent ventures/prototypes/paid experiments?

Create a versioned owner policy and record an AuditEvent. Do not infer unanswered values. LIVE spend remains zero until explicit authorization.
