# Empirical Operating Program

Architecture does not assume the following answers. The company measures them continuously.

## Programs

1. **Model by role/task:** store provider/model, cost, latency, schema validity, independent review score and downstream outcome; periodically re-route.
2. **Specialist contribution:** measure decision/evidence/experiment quality and later venture outcomes by specialist contribution, not message count.
3. **Idea quality:** novelty, research survival, customer reaction, commercial commitment and economic value.
4. **Contrarian value:** material risks found, refinements adopted, losses avoided, false kills and time/capital saved.
5. **Experiment information value:** uncertainty reduction per dollar/time and downstream decision impact.
6. **Acquisition channel:** conversion, CAC, sales cycle, contribution margin and retention by channel.
7. **Local-model viability:** benchmark on real role workloads before substitution.
8. **Cross-venture learning:** compare later cohorts after Skills/priors change; preserve control/baseline where practical.
9. **Profitability:** real revenue, cost, margin, payback, retention and cash; never substitute internal scores.

Every material prediction should have outcome definition, probability and review horizon so calibration/Brier/proper scoring is possible.
