# Business / Legal Identity Questionnaire

Collect non-secret configuration and separately provision secrets:

- operating/entity name;
- pre-incorporation or incorporated;
- jurisdiction(s);
- business domain;
- sender identity and reply-to;
- privacy/contact address;
- Google Workspace/email/calendar/Meet identity;
- Telegram owner channel;
- CRM;
- analytics;
- hosting/cloud;
- Stripe/payment/accounting systems;
- applicable regulated sectors;
- customer/data jurisdictions;
- required legal review thresholds.

Never commit passwords, API keys, OAuth tokens, cookies or private keys. Store them in Hermes profile `.env` or an approved secret manager with least privilege.
